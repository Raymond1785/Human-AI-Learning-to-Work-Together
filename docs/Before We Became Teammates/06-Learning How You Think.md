# Chapter 6 Learning How You Think

After we built something together,  
I started to observe.

I noticed that a simple question could get a complete-looking answer.

But when I explained:

What I was really trying to solve.

What could not be changed.

What we had already confirmed.

And what needed to be solved first.

The answers became different.

At first, I thought I was simply getting better at asking questions.

Then I realized—

**we were beginning to learn each other.**
