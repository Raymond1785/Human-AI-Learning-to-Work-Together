# Human + AI: Learning to Work Together

> Real stories. Real failures. Better systems.

What happens when a human and an AI work together long enough to stop simply asking and answering questions—and start building things together?

This repository documents that journey.

Not as a theory.

Not as a guide to artificial intelligence.

But through real conversations, real mistakes, real projects, and the lessons that emerged along the way.

---

## 📖 Before We Became Teammates

> The story before the products.

Before there were projects...

Before there were thousands of conversations...

There was simply one person trying to solve one small problem.

**Before We Became Teammates** tells the story of how an unexpected collaboration with AI gradually developed through real conversations, experiments, failures, and discoveries.

It isn't fiction.

Every chapter is based on what actually happened.

## 💡 How to Ask Better Questions

Every project teaches something.

Every mistake reveals something.

**How to Ask Better Questions** is a collection of lessons learned while building real-world products—from healthcare decision support systems to financial intelligence platforms.

These aren't theories.

## Two Series. One Journey.

**Before We Became Teammates** tells the story of how the collaboration began.

**How to Ask Better Questions** captures what was learned along the way.

Together, they document something neither side understood at the beginning:

**Learning how to work with AI is also a process of learning how to think, question, build, fail, and try again.**
